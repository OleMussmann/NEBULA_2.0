Exercise 1: Compare Different LLM Models (15 mins)

  • Discuss with two generative AI models at the same time, use arena.ai in battle mode — two AIs answer the same prompt, you vote for the better response.
  • Indicate after each round which one is better
    • If one “wins” a round, it’s answers will be used as context for the follow-up questions for both models
    • Be aware that they will be very confident in their approaches. Make sure to understand and judge every aspect of the discussion.

Round 1 — "Easy"

I plan to wash my car. The car wash is 50m away. Should I take the car or go by foot?

Round 2 — "Complex"

I have 10GB of spectral measurement data I'd like to analyze. Which programming language should I use, and why?

This is intentionally incomplete — iterate, add constraints, push for a concrete architecture.

Reflection — What are the AIs doing well? Where do they struggle?
